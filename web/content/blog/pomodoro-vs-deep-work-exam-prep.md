---
title: 'Pomodoro vs deep work for exam prep'
description: 'Choosing between short bursts and long sessions when stakes are high.'
date: 2026-02-02
---

Both methods protect attention from noise. Pomodoro helps you start when you feel resistance. Deep work helps when context switching is expensive.

For exam weeks, mix them. Use shorter bursts early in the term to build volume, then switch to seventy-five to ninety minute blocks in the final stretch when synthesis matters.

Track post-session energy. If Pomodoro leaves you scattered, lengthen sessions. If deep blocks leave you depleted, insert micro-walks instead of feeds.

Neither method fixes chronic sleep debt. Pick the approach that keeps you honest without turning into a moral scoreboard.

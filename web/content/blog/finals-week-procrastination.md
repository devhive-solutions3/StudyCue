---
title: 'How to avoid procrastination during finals week'
description: 'Lower the activation energy without lowering standards.'
date: 2026-02-08
---

Procrastination spikes when tasks feel poorly defined. Convert every study task into an observable verb and a fifteen-minute spine.

Schedule recovery explicitly; guilt-driven all-nighters create more avoidance next round.

Celebrate closed loops—even small—more than heroic marathons nobody can repeat.

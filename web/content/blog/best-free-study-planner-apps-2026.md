Notion is powerful but easy to over-build. Google Calendar wins for shared infrastructure but is thin on task semantics. Todoist captures fast but may nudge you toward paid filters as courseload grows.

StudyCue stays local-first on mobile with an optional cloud mirror for classes, tasks, and focus sessions. Pair it with whatever calendar your school mandates; the win is one place that understands both time blocks and graded work.

Judge apps on export paths and offline behavior. If you cannot leave with your data, you do not own your plan.

---
title: 'Note taking systems compared for STEM lectures'
description: 'Cornell, skeletal outlines, and problem-led notes.'
date: 2026-02-10
---

Cornell works when you review the cue column systematically. Skeleton outlines excel when proofs build linearly. Problem-led notes start from representative exercises and annotate theory backward.

Rotate systems by course type instead of debating which is universally best.

The metric is rework time before exams, not prettiness mid-semester.

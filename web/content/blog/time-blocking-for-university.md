---
title: 'Time blocking for university students with weird gaps'
description: 'Using awkward calendar holes without losing deep work.'
date: 2026-02-07
---

Weird gaps reward prep, not doom-scrolling. Label each campus hole with a default: review, chores, or true rest.

Stack shallow admin into the smallest gap blocks. Protect two deep anchors weekly even if they move between library rooms.

Treat block moves as signal: your estimate was wrong, adjust the template—not your self-worth.

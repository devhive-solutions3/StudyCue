---
title: 'How to study for AP Calculus without drowning in practice tests'
description: 'Split mechanics practice from interpretation-heavy problems.'
date: 2026-02-05
---

Calculus punishes sloppy algebra and fuzzy limit reasoning differently. Alternate days instead of chaining full-length practice exams.

Track error classes—chain rule slips, forgotten domains, bungled units—and drill buckets for twenty minutes daily. Breadth arrives on weekends.

Teaching the graph-story of each derivative rule out loud once per unit beats silently re-reading.

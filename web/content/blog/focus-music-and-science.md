---
title: 'Does focus music work? Practical read'
description: 'When playlists help and when silence wins.'
date: 2026-02-13
---

Lyrics compete with verbal working memory. Instrumentals or predictable noise sometimes mask distractions in open offices or dining halls.

If your error rate climbs with tracks, revert to silence for proof-heavy work.

Match audio to sensory needs, not aesthetic identity.

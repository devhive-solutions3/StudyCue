---
title: 'Active recall beats passive re-reading — protocol'
description: 'A weekly cadence so flashcards stay honest.'
date: 2026-02-06
---

Re-reading feels productive because fluency spikes. Exams measure retrieval under pressure, which is a different pathway.

After each lecture, write three questions you could not answer cold. Schedule them two days later. Missed questions become your only flashcard queue.

If cards feel easy, switch prompts to explain-why variants, not more volume.

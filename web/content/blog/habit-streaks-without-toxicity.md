---
title: 'Study streaks without shame spirals'
description: 'Design forgiving metrics that tolerate real life.'
date: 2026-02-11
---

Binary streaks break when labs run late or health wobbles. Track rolling seven-day adherence instead.

Define minimum viable sessions—twenty-five focused minutes—to keep identity intact on bad days.

If you miss twice in a row, shrink scope before you reboot entirely. Progress compounds from smaller wins.

---
title: 'How AI study assistants really work (and where they fail)'
description: 'Why models help with planning language and fail with uncited facts.'
date: 2026-02-04
---

Large language models are statistical pattern matchers. They are strong at reframing, prioritization language, and surfacing schedule conflicts. They are weak on course-specific truth unless you paste the syllabus yourself.

Fast models feel snappy; larger models add nuance. The healthy product pattern is human ground truth plus AI proposals you explicitly accept.

Responsible assistants help you plan and sequence work—not complete graded exercises. Anything else is an academic integrity trap.
